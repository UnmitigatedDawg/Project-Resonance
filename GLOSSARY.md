# Project Resonance Glossary

This glossary defines the common terminology used throughout Project Resonance.

Its purpose is to ensure that contributors, designers, developers, and future collaborators share a common understanding of the project's language.

Terms are grouped by category and will evolve as the project grows.

---

# Product Terms

## Play Mode

The primary user interface used while playing guitar.

Play Mode emphasizes performance, quick interaction, and minimal distraction.

---

## Tone Studio

The editing environment where tones, pedals, amplifiers, cabinets, and signal chains are created and modified.

---

## Tone

A complete guitar sound configuration including amplifier settings, cabinet selection, effects, signal chain, and associated metadata.

---

## Tone Profile

A Tone enriched with additional information such as:

- Guitar used
- Pickup selection
- Guitar volume and tone settings
- Inspiration notes
- Voice-to-text notes
- Tags
- Version history
- AI-generated cover art (future release)

---

## Pedalboard

The visual representation of the active signal chain.

Only active devices are displayed in Play Mode.

The complete editable signal chain is available in Tone Studio.

---

## Dynamic Information Card

A compact information panel displaying context about the currently selected Tone, such as:

- Guitar
- Pickup selection
- Tone controls
- Connection status
- Other relevant performance information

---

## Snapshot

A manually created version of a Tone Profile that preserves a specific point in its evolution.

Snapshots are user initiated rather than automatically generated.

---

# Design Terms

## Progressive Disclosure

A design principle that reveals complexity only when the user needs it.

Play Mode focuses on performance.

Tone Studio focuses on editing.

---

## Creative Flow

The uninterrupted mental state in which a musician remains focused on creating music rather than operating software.

Protecting the creative flow is one of the primary goals of Project Resonance.

---

## Resonance Test

A simple question used to evaluate new ideas:

> **Does this make it easier to create music?**

If the answer is **no**, the idea should be reconsidered, deferred, or rejected.

The Resonance Test serves as the primary product philosophy check throughout Project Resonance.

---

## Instrument First

A guiding philosophy that the application should feel like an extension of the instrument rather than a separate piece of software.

---

# Engineering Terms

## Product Bible

The collection of documents that define the vision, philosophy, standards, and long-term direction of Project Resonance.

---

## Architectural Decision Record (ADR)

A document that records significant technical architecture decisions and the reasoning behind them.

---

## Design Decision Log (DDL)

A record of important user experience and interface design decisions.

---

## Product Decision Log (PDL)

A record of product-level decisions affecting features, priorities, and user workflows.

---

## Product Standard (PS)

A documented standard that guides consistency throughout the product.

---

## Repository Bootstrap

The initial creation and organization of the Project Resonance GitHub repository.

---

# Project Terms

## Sprint

A planned body of work organized around a specific deliverable rather than a fixed duration.

---

## Milestone

A significant project objective marking measurable progress.

---

## Blueprints

The name of Milestone 1.2.

Repository Bootstrap and documentation foundation.

---

## The Book

The name of Milestone 1.3.

Creation of the Product Bible.

---

# Living Document

This glossary is expected to evolve throughout the life of Project Resonance.

New terminology should be added whenever a new concept becomes part of the project vocabulary.
