# Product Philosophy

> **Every feature should serve the musician before it serves the technology.**

Project Resonance exists to support musicians, not distract them.

Every design decision, engineering decision, and product decision should make it easier for a guitarist to create, capture, and recreate great music.

Technology exists to serve creativity, never to compete with it.

---

# Our Beliefs

## Music Comes First

The purpose of Project Resonance is not to showcase technology.

Its purpose is to help musicians create music.

Whenever technology competes with creativity, creativity should win.

---

## Protect the Creative Flow

Inspiration is often brief.

Interruptions matter.

Project Resonance should reduce friction between an idea and the ability to hear it, save it, and return to it later.

The application should help preserve moments of inspiration rather than interrupt them.

---

## Feel Like an Instrument

Project Resonance should feel like an extension of the guitarist's instrument.

Interactions should feel natural, immediate, and predictable.

The software should disappear into the playing experience.

---

## Simplicity is Strength

Simple does not mean limited.

Simple means thoughtful.

Complexity should exist only where it provides genuine value.

Features should earn their place within the application.

---

## Capture More Than Settings

A great guitar tone is more than amplifier settings.

It is the combination of equipment, technique, inspiration, and context.

Project Resonance should preserve the story behind a tone, not just the numbers that created it.

---

## Design for Real Musicians

Every workflow should begin with a simple question:

"What is the guitarist trying to accomplish right now?"

The answer should guide the design.

Interfaces should support musicians of every experience level without becoming overwhelming.

---

## Build for the Long Term

Project Resonance should remain understandable, maintainable, and adaptable.

Quick solutions that create long-term complexity should be avoided whenever practical.

Good architecture protects future creativity as much as good design protects today's creativity.

---

# The Resonance Test

Every significant feature, workflow, or design decision should be evaluated using one question:

> **Does this make it easier to create music?**

If the answer is no, the idea should be reconsidered, refined, deferred, or rejected.

The Resonance Test is the primary philosophical check for Project Resonance.

---

# Philosophy in Practice

The philosophy of Project Resonance influences every aspect of the product.

It explains why:

- Play Mode emphasizes performance over editing.
- Tone Studio provides powerful editing without cluttering the performance experience.
- Tone Profiles capture inspiration as well as settings.
- Progressive Disclosure reduces cognitive load.
- Documentation precedes implementation.
- Decisions are recorded so future contributors understand not only what was built, but why.

---

# Looking Forward

As Project Resonance evolves, new features and technologies will emerge.

The philosophy should remain stable.

Features may change.

Technology may change.

The commitment to musicians should not.

---

## Decisions

- Product Philosophy established.
- The Resonance Test adopted as the primary product evaluation principle.
- Creative Flow recognized as a foundational design objective.
- Instrument First adopted as a guiding interaction philosophy.
