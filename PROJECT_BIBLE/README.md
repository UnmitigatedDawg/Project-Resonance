# Project Resonance Product Bible

Welcome to the Product Bible.

The Product Bible is the authoritative source for the vision, philosophy, design principles, standards, and long-term direction of Project Resonance.

Every significant product decision should align with the guidance documented here.

---

# Purpose

The Product Bible exists to preserve the thinking behind Project Resonance.

While source code explains *how* the application works, the Product Bible explains *why* it was built the way it was.

Its purpose is to ensure that the product remains consistent as it evolves over time.

The Product Bible documents decisions that should outlive individual implementations.

---

# Organization

The Product Bible is organized into chapters.

Each chapter focuses on a specific aspect of the product.

Current chapters include:

- 00 – Cover
- 01 – Mission and Vision

Additional chapters will be added as Milestone 1.3 ("The Book") progresses.

---

# Guiding Principles

The Product Bible should:

- Explain decisions rather than merely record them.
- Capture enduring principles rather than temporary implementation details.
- Be reviewed whenever significant product direction changes.
- Remain understandable to both technical and non-technical contributors.

---

# Relationship to Other Documentation

The Product Bible works together with the rest of the repository.

- **README.md** introduces the project.
- **GETTING_STARTED.md** explains how to navigate the repository.
- **CONTRIBUTING.md** explains how contributors work together.
- **The Product Bible** explains the product itself.
- **ADRs** explain technical architecture decisions.
- **DDLs** explain design decisions.
- **PDLs** explain product decisions.

Together, these documents provide a complete understanding of Project Resonance.

---

# Living Documentation

The Product Bible is a living document.

It will continue to grow alongside the product.

New chapters may be added as the project evolves, provided they contribute to a clearer understanding of the product's vision and philosophy.
