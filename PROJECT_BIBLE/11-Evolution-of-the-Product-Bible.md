# Evolution of the Product Bible

> **The Product Bible is a living guide. It should evolve with the product while preserving the thinking that shaped it.**

The Product Bible exists to capture the purpose, philosophy, standards, and direction of Project Resonance.

As the project grows, this document should continue to grow alongside it.

Its purpose is not merely to record decisions, but to preserve the reasoning behind those decisions for future contributors.

---

# A Living Document

The Product Bible is intended to evolve.

New chapters, revisions, and refinements are expected as Project Resonance matures.

Changes should improve clarity without losing the original intent of the project.

---

# Preserve the Story

Every meaningful change represents another chapter in the project's history.

Major milestones should be documented so future contributors understand not only what changed, but why.

The journey is part of the project's identity.

---

# Respect the Foundation

The Mission, Product Philosophy, Core Values, and Design Principles provide the foundation of Project Resonance.

These foundational chapters should change only when there is a compelling reason.

Stability creates consistency.

Consistency builds trust.

---

# Improve Through Experience

Experience should shape future revisions.

Feedback from musicians, contributors, testing, and real-world use should continually inform improvements to both the product and its documentation.

Learning is expected.

Continuous improvement is encouraged.

---

# Version the Documentation

Documentation should evolve with the same discipline applied to software.

Meaningful revisions should:

- Update the version.
- Record the date of change.
- Explain significant revisions.
- Maintain a clear history of the document's evolution.

Documentation deserves the same level of care as implementation.

---

# Welcome Future Contributors

Every contributor brings new ideas and perspectives.

The Product Bible should provide enough context that future contributors can understand the project's direction without needing to rediscover its purpose.

Clear documentation reduces barriers to contribution.

---

# Protect the Vision

As new technologies, ideas, and features emerge, Project Resonance should continue asking one question:

> **Does this strengthen the musician's experience while remaining true to our philosophy?**

Growth should expand the project without changing its identity.

---

# Stewardship

Every contributor becomes a steward of Project Resonance.

Stewardship means improving the product while respecting the work that came before and preserving a strong foundation for those who follow.

The goal is not simply to add features, but to leave the project better than it was found.

---

# Decisions

- Product Bible recognized as a living document.
- Documentation versioning adopted.
- Stewardship established as an ongoing responsibility.
- Historical context recognized as part of the project's value.
