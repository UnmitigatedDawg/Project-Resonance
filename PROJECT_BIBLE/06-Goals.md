# Goals

> **Great products are built with purpose. Great teams measure their progress against that purpose.**

The goals of Project Resonance define the outcomes the project seeks to achieve.

They provide direction for planning, prioritization, and measuring success while remaining aligned with the project's Mission, Product Philosophy, and Core Values.

---

# Product Goals

## Create an Exceptional Playing Experience

Provide a fast, intuitive, and enjoyable companion application that feels like an extension of the musician's instrument.

---

## Preserve Musical Inspiration

Enable musicians to capture not only amplifier settings but also the context, inspiration, and equipment that made a tone meaningful.

---

## Reduce Friction

Design workflows that minimize unnecessary steps and keep musicians focused on playing rather than managing software.

---

## Build User Confidence

Deliver a predictable, reliable experience that users can trust during practice, rehearsal, and performance.

---

# Engineering Goals

## Maintain High Quality

Favor thoughtful engineering, maintainable architecture, and thorough documentation over rapid feature expansion.

---

## Build for Growth

Design the application so new capabilities can be added without introducing unnecessary complexity.

---

## Preserve Knowledge

Document important decisions so future contributors understand both the implementation and the reasoning behind it.

---

# Community Goals

## Welcome Contributors

Maintain clear documentation, coding standards, and project organization that encourage future collaboration.

---

## Encourage Learning

Treat Project Resonance as both a product and a learning opportunity for contributors interested in software craftsmanship, product design, and user experience.

---

# Success Measures

Project Resonance should strive to:

- Minimize the time required to access and edit tones.
- Make Tone Profiles more valuable than traditional presets.
- Maintain consistent documentation alongside implementation.
- Deliver stable releases before expanding feature scope.
- Continuously improve the musician's experience.

---

# Decisions

- Long-term project goals established.
- Product, Engineering, and Community goals adopted.
- Success measures identified for future evaluation.
