# Sprint 01 Retrospective

> **"A strong foundation is invisible when everything works—but indispensable when challenges arise."**

Sprint 01 focused on creating the foundation for Project Resonance.

Rather than immediately writing software, the project invested in defining its purpose, philosophy, standards, architecture, workflows, and long-term direction.

The result is a repository that provides a clear vision for both product and engineering work before implementation begins.

---

# Sprint Goal

Establish the product, documentation, and engineering foundations necessary to support long-term development.

**Result:** Achieved.

---

# Major Accomplishments

During Sprint 01 we:

- Created the GitHub repository.
- Established the repository structure.
- Created the Product Bible.
- Defined the Mission and Vision.
- Established the Product Philosophy.
- Defined the Core Values.
- Documented the Design Principles.
- Established Product Standards.
- Defined project Goals.
- Created the Roadmap.
- Defined the Release Strategy.
- Captured "What We Believe."
- Documented the Story of Project Resonance.
- Established the long-term evolution of the Product Bible.
- Created the Development Log.
- Created the Timeline.
- Established documentation standards.
- Established Git standards.
- Created contribution guidelines.
- Established the Product, Design, and Engineering vocabulary.

---

# What Went Well

- Documentation-first development created exceptional clarity.
- Product decisions became easier over time because they were grounded in shared principles.
- GitHub proved to be an effective long-term home for the project.
- The Product Bible evolved into a comprehensive guide rather than a collection of notes.
- The collaboration between Product and Technical Architecture remained highly aligned throughout the sprint.

---

# Lessons Learned

- Investing in documentation early significantly reduced uncertainty.
- Good architecture begins with a shared understanding of the problem.
- Product philosophy should guide engineering decisions.
- Small, incremental commits produce a clear and understandable project history.
- Professional software practices scale well, even for a two-person project.

---

# What We Would Do Differently

If starting Sprint 01 again:

- Create the Product Bible structure earlier.
- Introduce repository standards sooner.
- Begin the Development Log from the first day.
- Separate Product and Engineering workstreams from the outset.

---

# Risks Removed

Sprint 01 substantially reduced uncertainty in:

- Product direction.
- User experience.
- Documentation standards.
- Repository organization.
- Development workflow.
- Long-term project vision.

The largest remaining technical risk is Bluetooth communication with the NUX Mighty Plug Pro MP-3.

During Sprint 01 it evolved into a thoughtfully designed software product with its own philosophy, identity, and long-term vision.

The project now has a foundation capable of supporting years of future development.

Sprint 01 is complete.
